FAQ

Soalan lazimFrequently asked questions

Penyewa perlu daftar atau download app?Do tenants need to sign up or download an app?

Tidak. Penyewa cuma buka link khas yang anda hantar. Mereka boleh pasang sebagai app (Android APK atau dari browser) jika mahu.

No. Tenants just open the private link you send. They can install it as an app (Android APK or from the browser) if they want.

Duit sewa masuk ke mana?Where does the rent money go?

Terus ke akaun bank anda sendiri. Penyewa bayar seperti biasa (DuitNow, pindahan bank, QR) dan muat naik slip. ProSewa tidak memegang wang anda.

Straight to your own bank account. Tenants pay as usual (DuitNow, bank transfer, QR) and upload the slip. ProSewa never holds your money.

Kalau penyewa tak pandai guna app?What if a tenant isn't good with apps?

Penyewa cuma hantar bukti bayaran kepada anda. Tekan Bayaran manual, pilih kaedah (pindahan bank, Touch 'n Go, deposit tunai, tunai) dan muat naik slip. Resit tetap dijana dan masuk ke laporan.

They just send you the proof. Tap Manual payment, choose the method (bank transfer, Touch 'n Go, cash deposit, cash) and upload the slip. A receipt is still issued and it goes into your reports.

Kalau penyewa terbayar lebih?What if a tenant overpays?

Lebihan dibawa ke bulan depan sebagai kredit secara automatik. Penyewa nampak nota bahawa lebihan akan ditolak daripada sewa bulan depan.

The extra is carried to next month as credit automatically. The tenant sees a note that it will be deducted from next month's rent.

Boleh guna untuk bilik sewa atau banyak rumah?Does it work for rooms or multiple houses?

Boleh. Daftar setiap rumah dan biliknya di tab Hartanah. Anda nampak bilik kosong, kadar penghunian dan sewa yang hilang, dan laporan cukai dipecahkan ikut rumah.

Yes. Register each house and its rooms in the Properties tab. You'll see vacant rooms, occupancy and lost rent, and the tax report is broken down by property.

Boleh rekod bil air, elektrik, IWK & maintenance?Can I record water, electricity, IWK & maintenance bills?

Boleh. Rekod beberapa bil sekali gus ikut rumah, atau tetapkan bil tetap (IWK, maintenance, internet) sebagai bil berulang yang direkod automatik setiap bulan. Semua bil ditolak dalam laporan pendapatan sewa bersih.

Yes. Record several bills at once per property, or set fixed bills (IWK, maintenance, internet) as recurring bills recorded automatically every month. All bills are deducted in the net rental income report.

Penyewa boleh hantar slip PDF?Can tenants send a PDF slip?

Boleh. Gambar, screenshot atau PDF dari app bank semuanya diterima. PDF ditukar kepada gambar supaya mudah disemak.

Yes. Images, screenshots or PDFs from the bank app are all accepted. PDFs are converted to an image so they're easy to review.

Laporan cukai ni untuk apa?What is the tax report for?

Ia mengira sewa kasar, perbelanjaan dan pendapatan sewa bersih setahun, sebagai rujukan untuk e-Filing LHDN dan ejen cukai anda.

It calculates gross rent, expenses and net rental income for the year, as a reference for LHDN e-Filing and your tax agent.

Data saya selamat?Is my data safe?

Data disimpan dalam Google Firebase. Setiap owner hanya nampak data sendiri, dan link penyewa boleh ditukar bila-bila masa jika tersebar.

Data is stored on Google Firebase. Each owner only sees their own data, and tenant links can be changed anytime if leaked.
