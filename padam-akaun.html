Padam Akaun ProSewa

ProSewa oleh KUKUSAN LEGACY

Anda boleh memadam akaun ProSewa dan semua data yang berkaitan pada bila-bila masa, tanpa perlu menghubungi kami.

Cara 1: Di sini (web)

  1. Tekan butang di bawah dan log masuk dengan akaun ProSewa anda.
  2. Taip PADAM dan masukkan kata laluan untuk mengesahkan.
  3. Akaun dan semua data dipadam serta-merta.
🗑 Log masuk & padam akaun

Cara 2: Dalam app ProSewa

Buka app → Tetapan → ⚠️ Zon bahaya → Padam akaun saya → taip PADAM & kata laluan.

Data yang dipadam

  • Akaun log masuk (emel & kata laluan)
  • Profil, maklumat syarikat, logo, maklumat bank & kod QR
  • Semua penyewa, bayaran, bukti bayaran, resit, deposit dan perjanjian sewa (termasuk tandatangan)
  • Aduan, peringatan, perbelanjaan, bil, rumah dan laporan masalah
  • Peranti notifikasi yang berdaftar

Link penyewa anda tidak boleh dibuka lagi selepas akaun dipadam.

Data yang disimpan

  • Rekod bayaran langganan (tarikh, pelan, amaun) disimpan untuk perakaunan dan cukai seperti yang dikehendaki undang-undang.
  • Salinan sandaran keselamatan dipadam secara automatik dalam tempoh 98 hari.

Baki langganan yang belum digunakan tidak dikembalikan. Tindakan ini tidak boleh dibatalkan, jadi muat turun laporan PDF anda dahulu jika perlu.

Penyewa

Penyewa tidak mempunyai akaun ProSewa. Untuk memadam data anda sebagai penyewa, minta tuan rumah anda memadam rekod penyewa anda dalam app, atau hubungi kami melalui tuan rumah anda.

Delete Your ProSewa Account

ProSewa by KUKUSAN LEGACY

You can delete your ProSewa account and all related data at any time, without contacting us.

Option 1: Here (web)

  1. Tap the button below and sign in with your ProSewa account.
  2. Type PADAM and enter your password to confirm.
  3. Your account and all data are deleted immediately.
🗑 Sign in & delete account

Option 2: In the ProSewa app

Open the app → Settings → ⚠️ Danger zone → Delete my account → type PADAM & your password.

Data that is deleted

  • Sign-in account (email & password)
  • Profile, company details, logo, bank details & QR code
  • All tenants, payments, payment proofs, receipts, deposits and tenancy agreements (including signatures)
  • Requests, reminders, expenses, bills, properties and problem reports
  • Registered notification devices

Your tenant links stop working once the account is deleted.

Data that is kept

  • Subscription payment records (date, plan, amount) are kept for accounting and tax purposes as required by law.
  • Security backup copies are deleted automatically within 98 days.

Unused subscription time is not refunded. This cannot be undone, so download your PDF reports first if needed.

Tenants

Tenants do not have a ProSewa account. To delete your data as a tenant, ask your landlord to delete your tenant record in the app, or contact us through your landlord.
